Privacy policy

Version of 13.11.2026. This is a translation for convenience; if it differs from the Russian version, the Russian version prevails.

1. Who processes the data

The personal data operator is [full name / sole proprietor / company, tax ID] (“we”). Fixum works as a Telegram bot and mini app. For questions about your data, contact support: message the bot or use “Support” in the app[, or e-mail].

2. What data we store

  • from Telegram: your ID, username (@username), name and Telegram interface language — Telegram passes them when you open the bot or the app;
  • the app language, if you chose it in your profile;
  • orders you post: title, description, budget, video requirements, category, deadline;
  • your applications: links to posts, view counts, earnings and statuses;
  • withdrawal requests: amount, wallet, status and the reason for rejection, if any;
  • your USDT (TRC20) wallet address for payouts, if you added it to your profile;
  • advertisers' ratings and reviews of creators, and the advertiser's consent to show a video in the creator's portfolio;
  • links to your social media, if you added them to your profile, and the code you put in a profile description to confirm the account is yours;
  • if you applied to become an advertiser — the e-mail and, if you gave it, the phone number from the application, so the team can contact you;
  • how many times people followed the product links in your videos — only a counter, without data about who followed them;
  • if you connected a TikTok, VK or Instagram account in your profile — the account's ID and name on the platform and access keys to it (encrypted), as well as views and likes of the videos you submitted for orders. We don't request your other videos, messages, friends or followers;
  • moderators' comments on orders and videos;
  • reports you sent: what about, the reason and the comment;
  • if your account is blocked — the date and reason;
  • the dates these records were created and changed.

We don't store your profile photo: when a profile is viewed we get it from Telegram, and only if your Telegram privacy settings allow it.

Conversations with support are kept in Telegram, in a private support chat, not in the Fixum database. Only you and moderators see your wallet address — it is not shown to advertisers.

We don't receive your phone number from Telegram, your contact list or conversations with other people.

On the website we set one cookie for sign-in (valid for 30 days). No advertising or analytics cookies. The mini app sends error reports to Sentry (error text and the screen it happened on) — without your data, cookies or request contents.

3. Why

  • to show orders and applications and connect advertisers with creators;
  • to moderate orders, videos, reviews and reports, and to protect against fraud;
  • to pay for accepted videos;
  • to count video views automatically on connected platforms and make sure a submitted video was posted from your account;
  • to answer support questions and send notifications to the bot chat;
  • for anonymised statistics of how the service works: how many orders and videos, how long moderation takes.

The legal basis is performing our agreement with you (Art. 6 Part 1 Clause 5 of Federal Law 152-FZ): the service doesn't work without this data. No advertising, profiling or selling of data.

4. Who sees the data

  • The other party sees only your name and photo from Telegram — not your @username or social media links: all communication and payments go through Fixum.
  • A creator's profile — rating, reviews, number of accepted videos and titles of portfolio works — is seen by advertisers the creator sent videos to. The review author's name and video links are not shown to them.
  • Only moderators see who sent a report — the person reported doesn't find out.
  • Fixum moderators and managers see orders, applications, profiles and reports in full.

Data is not passed to third parties, except Telegram, through which the service works, and cases required by law. Advertisers don't see your connected TikTok, VK or Instagram account; from the platform itself we only request statistics of your videos using the key you gave us.

5. Where and how long we store it

Data is stored on a server in [country, city / hosting provider]. We keep it while you use the service; after a deletion request we delete it within 30 days.

6. Your rights

You can ask what data about you is stored, correct it or delete it. Contact support — we'll reply within 10 business days. You can disconnect a connected platform account in your profile at any time — its access keys are deleted immediately; you can also revoke access in the platform's own settings. You can also file a complaint with Roskomnadzor.

7. Changes

A new version takes effect once published here; the version date is at the top of the page.
